About us

Trust and quality, since 1996.

ICT – Industrial Chemicals Trading is an exporter and importer of chemicals. Operating in the international market for almost 30 years, we connect origin and destination, offer innovative solutions to customers, open markets for manufacturers, and strengthen the ICT brand as a synonym for trust and quality.

Founded in 1996 in China by investors with extensive experience in the chemical products market, we began activities focused on the local market. As the company grew, our operations quickly expanded into the international market.

After years of operation, we built a platform of production, processing, warehousing and logistics. With our management team, technical personnel, complete industrial chain and rich network of relationships, ICT is committed to providing our customers with the best possible service.
