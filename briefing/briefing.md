## 5. Main Services

### Primary (strongest visual emphasis on homepage)
- **QUALITY** — Qualified suppliers, strict quality standards, reliable products and consistent performance.
- **GLOBAL SOURCING** — Broad network of international manufacturers, competitive products, alternative supply sources.
- **LOGISTICS** — Coordinate international shipments, efficient routes, reliable delivery schedules origin→destination.
- **FINANCIAL SERVICES** — Secure payment terms, flexible financing, commercial conditions for long-term partnerships.
- **REGULATORY SUPPORT** — Documentation, product compliance, import requirements, customs procedures.
- **MARKET INTELLIGENCE** — Up-to-date pricing, product availability, market trends, international supply conditions.

### Secondary (secondary section or dedicated page)
- **SUPPLY CHAIN MANAGEMENT** — Planning/coordination of purchases, shipments, inventory to reduce risk and prevent supply interruptions.
- **CUSTOMIZED SOLUTIONS** — Tailor-made sourcing, packaging, logistics, commercial solutions per customer.
- **TECHNICAL SUPPORT** — Product specs, technical documentation, samples, manufacturer communication.
- **LONG-TERM PARTNERSHIPS** — Reliable, mutually beneficial business relationships beyond individual transactions.
